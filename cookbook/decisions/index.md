# Decisions

- [[restart-the-port-from-isaiahs-clean-base|Restart the port from Isaiah's clean base]] — Restart from Isaiah's 32b3ec9 with reviewed reference reuse and the same layer topology; BC and RTX PRO 6000 verification follow. Task 1.1 rebuilds a standalone hash-pinned kernel: 59 engine tests, four replays/2,876 transitions, green preparation (769 Python cases), isolated Cargo checks and explicit vendored-style exceptions; checker hardened after verification round 1.
- [[v3-reuses-isaiah-infrastructure-with-kaggriculture-semantics|V3 reuses Isaiah infrastructure with Kaggriculture semantics]] — Deliver one complete final adaptation of the efficient Isaiah pipeline that respects Isaiah's own principles in their game form; partial mechanisms are not finished deliverables, and the rejected decoder remains historical.
- [[diagnose-the-mechanism-before-spending-on-runs|Diagnose the mechanism before spending on runs]] — Keep decisions reproducible, map game capabilities to executable mechanisms, and distinguish four failure classes before repairing.
- [[the-policy-is-stateless-and-observation-only|The policy is stateless and observation-only]] — Carry forward explicit current-observation tokens and the prohibition on opponent identity conditioning; Isaiah's StatelessTransformerV1, never his recurrent model, is the reference contract, with the same layer topology and only game I/O differing.
- [[throughput-means-correct-complete-work|Throughput means correct complete work]] — High throughput is required; preserve semantic parity and measure equivalent work rather than inferring gains from language or kernels.
- [[evaluation-preserves-generality-and-evidence|Evaluation preserves generality and evidence]] — Measure win rate and bank margin across opponents, retain source-bound evidence and telemetry, and keep submission authority with the owner.
- [[start-multi-gpu-qualification-with-two-ranks|Start multi-GPU qualification with two ranks]] — Qualify on two GPUs before four; 2/4-rank RTX PRO 6000 verification is authorized; keep the pod running after bounded runs; host history lives in ops.
- [[preserve-the-ppo-recipe-across-bc-bootstrap|Preserve the PPO recipe across BC bootstrap]] — The BC-period two-rank 4096-env fixed-minibatch recipe is preserved as a historical snapshot with separate checkpoint custody; later runs follow Isaiah alignment and teacher-adapter completion.
- [[recipe-choices-align-to-isaiah-without-owner-escalation|Recipe choices align to Isaiah without owner escalation]] — Optimizer-step cadence and critic/value-distillation semantics are implementer choices resolved toward Isaiah scaling_6m and its multi-GPU rule, not owner decisions; both are encoded in the rebuilt configs and model and pass CPU config/startup checks, while trainer integration and GPU qualification remain pending.
