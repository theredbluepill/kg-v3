## Eight ranks likely for the main run — owner, 2026-09-29

> we'd likely use 8Xrtx 6000 in main run, 2 rank for diagonose/building.

Source: `user-directive:2026-09-29:eight-rank-main-run-two-rank-diagnostics`. The owner said "likely"; this records a stated expectation, **not** a final adoption of eight ranks, of an 8-GPU pod or of any run.

- **Roles.** The current two-rank RTX PRO 6000 pod stays the host for smoke, diagnostics and building (plan Tasks 6.1–6.2). Eight RTX PRO 6000 GPUs are the likely main-run hardware, qualified in plan Task 6.3b. The four-rank step (6.3) stays optional between them.
- **Shape.** `configs/kaggriculture_8rank.yaml` applies Isaiah's 6M multi-GPU division (`winner_ce_6m_4x5090.yaml`; see the precedents below): 32 envs/rank, `segments_per_minibatch` 2, accumulation 1, so the global batch (256 envs), optimizer steps per iteration (16), global segments per step (16) and transitions per iteration (16,384) equal `scaling_6m`. The optimizer, schedule, PPO coefficients, compile settings and teacher settings are unchanged. `teacher_segments_per_minibatch` stays 128 as in Isaiah's per-rank configs; it is a precompute chunk size, so each rank precomputes its 32 envs in one chunk.
- **Checks on this version (CPU only).** `tests/kaggriculture/test_configs.py` derives every per-rank shape from `scaling_6m` divided by the world size and fails loudly when a global quantity, a per-rank minibatch or a teacher chunk is not a whole division (world sizes 3 and 32, accumulation 3 and teacher chunk 24 are rejected). It asserts the 8-rank global workload equals `scaling_6m`, and that the startup GEMM check sees 64 rollout, 256 minibatch, 4,096 teacher-chunk and 64 evaluation rows, one trunk and one head call each. `tests/scripts/test_run_ppo.py` drives `run_ppo.main` from the 8-rank YAML to the headroom lines and the explicit not-wired stop. Changing the YAML to spm 4 turned four config tests red and restoring it byte-for-byte turned them green (`ops/rebuild-2026-09-29/8rank/mutation-spm4.log`). `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 uvx --from rust-just just py-prepare` passed: ruff, format, mypy (62 files), 1,534 passed and 5 skipped (native grammar binding, two FlashAttention/CUDA, quantized backend, native Kaggriculture env), docs-fresh (`ops/rebuild-2026-09-29/8rank/py-prepare.log`). No GPU, Rust or training check ran.
- **Component estimate, not end-to-end.** The GPU checks bundle ("GPU checks bundle (component)" in `ops/rebuild-2026-09-29/results.md` on branch `kg/rebuild-gpu-checks`, ops evidence pending merge into this branch) timed the model alone at the 8-rank per-rank shape: 1.389 s (mid) and 2.372 s (dense) per update, or 11,796 and 6,906 global env steps/s if ranks scaled perfectly. That is 0.90 (mid) and 0.95 (dense) of four times the 2-rank rate. The smaller train-step batch is the common cost: at mid the rollout forwards and train steps add about equally (+0.295 s and +0.300 s), while the dense loss is train-step dominated (+0.572 s, rollout −0.063 s). It excludes the engine, host copies, GAE, logging and all-reduce. The timings ran alone on one GPU (GPU 0) of the two-GPU pod, with synthetic observations and fresh weights (the bundle as a whole used GPUs 0 and 1). It predicts no end-to-end SPS.
- **Open measurements (Task 6.3b).** The 8-GPU pod is a new billable resource: read and state its live price and get the owner's approval before creating it. Then measure, at 8-rank shapes: dense-state memory against the 85% target; complete-work SPS in env steps and learner turns, with phase costs; gradient all-reduce cost on the pod's actual interconnect (RTX PRO 6000 has no NVLink; read the topology rather than assume PCIe behavior); vCPUs per GPU against the Rust engine's step time and `native_threads`; the first-minibatch log-ratio alarm on every rank; W&B live sync; and seed-stream disjointness at world size 8 (the Task 1.4 L3 test includes world size 8).
- **Isaiah's multi-GPU precedents.** His only 8-GPU config is `configs/scaling_50m.yaml` (50M model, 8× B200, 4B env steps): 256 envs/rank, spm 8 × accumulation 2, horizon 64, so its global batch (2,048 envs) grows with the rank count, as `scaling_12m` (2× B200) and `scaling_25m` (4× B200) also keep 256 envs/rank. His 6M multi-GPU precedent, `configs/winner_ce_6m_4x5090.yaml`, instead divides `winner_ce_6m`'s `n_envs` and `segments_per_minibatch` by 4 and keeps its global batch. `kaggriculture_8rank.yaml` follows the 6M division because the goal is `scaling_6m`'s global workload at every rank count; there is no Isaiah 8-GPU 6M config, and `scaling_50m`'s growth comes with a larger model and budget, so it does not license enlarging a 6M batch.
- **Batch-size option (corrected 2026-09-29).** `winner_ce_6m` is **not** an Isaiah-aligned fallback for Kaggriculture. Relative to `scaling_6m`, `configs/winner_ce_6m.yaml` changes `rl.value_loss` to `winner_ce`, requires `env.reward_mode: win_only` and `value_mode: win_only` (model `stateless_transformer_6m_win_only`), uses a different LR schedule (warmup 200, decay 150,000, `lr_min_ratio` 0.02) and muon weight decay 0.05, and also sets `max_entities` 256, `two_player_weight` 0.5 and teacher chunk 256; its header calls it a value-loss ablation of `baseline.yaml`. Our economic reward is not a winner distribution, and the merged Task 3.2/3.4 guards in `python/owl/train/config.py` reject `winner_ce` and any reward mode other than `win_loss`. Two variants are recorded; neither is adopted:
  - **(c1) Full `winner_ce_6m` recipe:** a reward-design change (win-only reward, winner cross-entropy critic, its own schedule). It needs an owner decision and would reopen the reward and critic semantics, not just the config.
  - **(c2) Batch shape only:** 512 envs × horizon 128, spm 32 (64 envs/rank, spm 4 at 8 ranks) on `scaling_6m` settings. This matches neither Isaiah recipe: each of the 16 optimizer steps per iteration sees 4,096 transitions instead of 1,024 (65,536 per iteration instead of 16,384) under `scaling_6m`'s step-defined schedule. It is a recipe deviation, so adopting it requires a measured comparison against the divided `scaling_6m` shape (complete-work throughput and learning at equal env steps), recorded in the [[recipe-choices-align-to-isaiah-without-owner-escalation|alignment Decision]]. Low GPU utilization at 32 envs/rank is the trigger to measure, not a reason to switch.
