# BC bootstrap uses native replay features and current heads

## Mechanism diagnosis

The current policy and initial incumbent both end at zero banks in8 native games/seeds7000–7007,16 seat trajectories each. Current-policy mean bank falls from3000 to595.125 after one turn,26.5625 after eight, and zero by192; the initial policy shows the same failure pattern. Removing market orders while retaining model unit commands preserves3000 for every seat through719 turns, as does full PASS. This localizes the observed cash depletion to market behavior rather than a zeroed bank metric. It does not establish an optimal no-trade policy or full engine correctness. Diagnostic action sampling is eager BF16, not a compiled-path numerical parity or speed test.

Both-zero terminal W/L/D gives no ranking signal between these policies. Econ.2 is a capped death-potential difference, not a penalty for arbitrary spending or a bank-profit target. The current-policy diagnostic also accumulates8193 ineffective unit commands; economic production chains remain unlearned. Prior cookbook search found no qualified v3 BC implementation; v2's public replay loader provides observation/action alignment and shared-field semantics, not its model, collector or training code.
