Date: 2026-09-29. Scope: offline source investigation for stream D, before the
engine is vendored. Target: recommend 3–5 behaviorally different opponents with
an explicit dependency closure. Stop when the recommendation, source evidence,
size inventory and import/verification gaps are reviewable. No bot implementation,

## Recommendation

Select **starter, r04, ecobot and e776** for the first evaluation import review.
This is an implementer recommendation based on source structure and intended
behavior, not owner adoption, a strength ranking or completed Task 7.1. The four
cover a simple stationary crop baseline, adaptive task assignment, an economic
investment evaluator with day routes, and a repaired prerecorded production
program. The pair r04/ecobot both adapt production, but their decision mechanisms
are different; measured action/economic diversity remains to be checked.

`kg/reference-2026-09-29` at **65f0eac5bb00b18a9d3acce319c2a231cbd5dff0**.

### Styles and source evidence

**Starter — stationary carrot cycle / sparse baseline.** `starter.rs:1–7`,
`carrot_rule` at 62–119 and `StarterController::action` at 192–217 buy a carrot
seed, plant under the farmer, water, harvest at age three and sell shed carrots.
It never hires or moves and always returns an empty hands list. This is useful
for identifying whether a learned policy can realize a simple crop payoff;
it is not a competitive qualification opponent by itself. It is behaviorally
stateless; `last_debug` is diagnostic only. The source assumes 24 turns/day and
hardcodes carrot cost/maturity (14–16).

**R04 — diversified production with per-turn job assignment and price floors.**
`r04.rs:update_targets` (732–810) adjusts cows/sheep/geese and crop targets using
shop demand, day, money and market inventory. `build_jobs` (983 onward),
`global_assignment_pairs` (1409 onward), and `assign` (1500 onward) allocate units
to maintenance/production work rather than replaying a tape. `market_orders`
(1759 onward) reserves feed/cash, sells above product-specific floors, handles
shed overflow, hires and expands land. `action` (2139–2169) replans day roles and
builds fresh jobs each turn. This adds labor-allocation and multi-product pressure.
It retains roles, targets and assignment state across turns.

**EcoBot v7 — expected-profit investment, herd culling and daily route insertion.**
`ecobot.rs:evaluate_animal` (1847 onward) estimates revenue, fertilizer, feed,
labor and net present value; crop evaluators follow. `update_drift` (1778 onward)
uses observed market inventory changes; `update_cull` (2008 onward) retains or
reduces herd caps. `evaluate_turn` (2245 onward) combines current shops, prices,
stock and census. `eval_route` (1143 onward), `plan_routes` (1355 onward) and
`solve_day` (1508 onward) insert tasks into daily worker routes subject to time
and resource budgets. This specifically exercises investment/payback and market
adaptation beyond R04's target schedules. It owns drift/cull history and consumes
route queues; it is not an example architecture for the stateless learned policy.

**E776 — frozen Kenjo production program with execution repair and demand response.**
`e776.rs:1–7`, `pressure` (302 onward), `assign_bundle` (317 onward), and `action`
(897–929) show a frozen action program modified by cow/sheep substitution from
visible wool/dairy demand, weed repair, sale caps/slot ordering, funding checks,
hand alignment, terminal liquidation and latent-pasture delivery. This supplies
a timing/production-program opponent with a much smaller data dependency than
the multi-route families. It retains transaction/bundle/activation state.

## Deferred alternatives and the dependency trap

This review inspected all native-agent module headers/imports/embedded-file
references, plus the selected controllers' decision paths. The reference enum
in `native_agents/mod.rs` has **20 controller kinds**, despite C2's parenthetical
“22 scripted bots”; `jsonl` and `library` are support modules, not extra registered
opponents. Use the enum and explicit dependency closure when importing.

- **V43:** seven files, 229,721 bytes / 6,408 lines before transitive dependencies,
  plus `v43-routes.json` at 4,866,690 bytes. It is not independent:
  `v43/core.rs:249` calls `farm2945::util::py_round3`, and
  `v43/production.rs:1052,1117,1124,1475` calls `farm2945::sheep` helpers.
  Those helpers reference V43 again. A naive import of the Farm2945 parent module
  drags in its remaining submodules as well.
- **Farm2945:** ten files, 149,183 bytes / 4,097 lines before V43 and other support.
  `farm2945/mod.rs:22` depends on `V43Controller`; `race.rs:209–214` embeds both
  `farm2945-sell-library.bin` (1,352,314 bytes) and `metav4-sell-library.bin`
  (1,319,827 bytes), and references Metav4's library lengths. Runtime branches do
  not remove compile-time `include_bytes!` dependencies. Its herd/race layers are
  a possible later challenge opponent if a panel lacks that pressure.
- **Cha22:** six files including execution tests, 94,143 bytes / 2,392 lines
  before its parents. `cha22/mod.rs:10`, `early.rs:3–7`, and `tail.rs:7–17` depend
  on Metav4, Farm2945, Pipe16, V43 and V47. Metav4 in turn uses V48 compaction.
  Its small top-level module is not its import footprint.
- **Shop Router:** independently scoped source (22,383 bytes), but its 13-tape
  `shop-router-0909-actions.json` is 5,099,830 bytes. It has a tracked dedicated
  parity test and fixture, which is an advantage when revisiting it. Defer it
  for the initial footprint; E776 already supplies a repaired tape policy.
- **Evgen, Flex, Tetsutani:** independent tape-based alternatives; their policy
  data are 109,733, 687,817 and 460,067 bytes respectively. Evgen could replace
  E776 to emphasize clone/front-running behavior, but adding both initially gives
  less mechanism coverage than retaining both adaptive planners. This is source
  reasoning, not a measured redundancy claim.

## Import and evaluation contract

1. Keep selected V files and E776 data byte-exact with a dedicated manifest.
   Implement a small v3-owned registry for only the four variants; do not copy
   `native_agents/mod.rs` wholesale, since its module declarations pull in every
   controller. Preserve original license/attribution material separately from
   the runner. The reference engine has `engine_rs/LICENSE`, but source comments
   naming `agents/r04/main.py` and `agents/ecobot/main.py` point to paths absent
   from this reference Git tree. The corresponding local
   `/Users/poonszesen/kaggriculture-v2/agents/{r04,ecobot,e776}` directories were
   also absent in this checkout environment. Original upstream notices and exact
   differential oracles therefore remain a custody question before importing;
   comments that claim earlier parity are not new verification.
2. Use one fresh controller per **environment, seat and episode**. These objects
   cannot be shared across vector environments or both seats: R04, EcoBot and
   E776 hold a single state, not seat-indexed state. Reset on every auto-reset.
   Invoke `action` exactly once per turn; EcoBot consumes its route queue, and
   E776 resets on a repeated step (`e776.rs:898–901`). R04/EcoBot reset on a
   rewind/step zero, which is not a substitute for explicit lifecycle ownership.
3. Preserve the observation boundary. Although the legacy signatures accept
   `&Game`, selected paths read public facts and the acting seat's private state;
   they do not need RNG state or rival-private inventories. Verify this with
   hidden-state perturbation tests or a seat-visible reconstruction adapter.
   `native_agents/jsonl.rs:1–5` describes the latter precedent, but the old JSONL
   CLI and all-bot dispatcher are not necessary dependencies. Opponent identity,
   controller memory and debug data stay exclusively in evaluator bookkeeping;
   none enters the learned actor/critic, normalization, rewards or checkpoint
   selection. External scripted controller state does not authorize learned
   policy memory.
4. Start qualification on the pinned **default game configuration**, with its
   719 executable transitions. These bots hardcode 24 turns/day, 30 days, fixed
   day/step boundaries and other default rules; contract v4 permits a wider
   configuration envelope. Do not claim bot support for every legal v4 config,
   silently reinterpret those constants, or truncate their official JSON actions
   to make them pass the model grammar. Any action-support mismatch is an
   explicit adaptation/qualification gap, including omitted hands and quantities.
5. Supply official `farmer`/`hands`/`market` JSON through the native engine's
   action path. Preserve market order and zero-quantity behavior. Record the
   applied action and verify execution/legality independently of an opponent's
   own projected-shed or funding heuristic. Use the shared v3 engine and
   evaluation infrastructure, never the old JA rollout/training machinery.
6. Before claiming parity, obtain/freeze observation-to-action oracle cases for
   both seats covering openings, day resets, weeds, market shortages, hires,
   late liquidation and mid-episode replay. R04/EcoBot/E776 have no inline
   `#[test]` cases in the pinned files and no dedicated tracked tests in
   `engine_rs/tests`; Starter has five inline cases. Reference parity comments
   and Starter's historical tests were inspected, **not executed here**.
   Then run bounded unit/action parity checks; full panel runs belong on the pod
   under the plan's run-statement rules.
7. Panel reports must keep version/hash, seeds, both seats, games attempted and
   completed, legality/failures, win rate, raw-bank margin and runtime. Fix the
   selection versus held-out split before collecting results and reserve fresh
   seeds/opponents for qualification. This brief assigns no panel roles and
   changes no last-best promotion rule. A stationary baseline plus three styles
   cannot alone establish public-ladder robustness.
