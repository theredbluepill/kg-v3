## Topology (Isaiah's actor blocks; the heads are the only game-specific part)

- **`actor_input_proj: nn.Linear(3D, D)`** over `[entity ‖ self player ‖ plan]`, Isaiah's `source_actor_input_proj` pattern. For unit frames, entity is `own_actor_hidden[:, a]`. For market frames, entity is the plan hidden (there's no market entity), as in the reference. The projection is **Muon-eligible**, as in Isaiah's model.
- `actor: KaggricultureGrammarActor(nn.Module)` holds everything game-specific:
  - `source_norm: LayerNorm` (Isaiah's actor normalizes its input)
  - `market_position: nn.Embedding(11, D)` (queue position; the actor's input layer)
  - `slot_embeddings: nn.ModuleDict` for within-frame prefix conditioning: `unit_kind 20`, `unit_item 16`, `unit_quantity_present 2`, `unit_quantity_high 32`, `market_kind 8`, `market_item 16`, `market_quantity_high 32`
  - `heads: nn.ModuleDict` of `OutputProjectionMLP(trunk_cfg, width)` for the 9 sampled slots (`unit_kind`, `unit_item`, `unit_quantity_present`, `unit_quantity_high`, `unit_quantity`, `market_kind`, `market_item`, `market_quantity_high`, `market_quantity`)
  - `unit_actor` is implicit (frame order), `unit_target` is reserved (always 0, log-prob 0), and `stop` is implicit (the first final market `NONE`)
- **Conditioning:** `hidden_stage = base + prefix / sqrt(stage + 1)`, where `prefix` sums the embeddings of the already-chosen slots in the frame. This is the reference's factorization, rebuilt.
- **Muon / initialization:**
  - `get_input_layers` adds `market_position` and the slot embeddings (learned input tables, excluded like Isaiah's tokens)
  - `get_output_layers` adds each head's `.out` (Isaiah's actor-head gain 0.01)
  - `actor_input_proj` and the heads' `.up` use Muon

## Sampling and replay semantics (contract v4)

- **Masks:** from a typed `GrammarTables` interface (`unit_kind [20]`, `unit_item[kind] [16]`, `unit_quantity_present[kind] [2]`, `unit_quantity_high[quantity_present] [32]`, `unit_quantity_low[high, zero] [32]`, `market_kind [8]`, `market_item[kind] [16]`, `market_quantity[kind] [32]`). `python/owl/kaggriculture/gpu_grammar.py` is ported from `gpu_sampling_grammar.py` (C7) and built from the native plans (C3, Task 1.2).
- **Unit frames:** one per observed own actor; slots are sampled in the fixed stage order `1, 3, 4, 5, 6`.
- **Market frames:** positions `0..10`; available iff `position < order_limits`.
  - **Kind:** Gumbel-max over the kind mask. HIRE capacity uses the **same Gumbel noise**: `counts + prior_hires < hire_limit`, with the corrected argmax.
  - **Replay** uses the final kinds with the capacity-corrected mask.
  - **STOP** is at the first final `NONE`; later positions are marginalized (zero log-prob).
- **Exact categorical sampling** via Gumbel-max, no multinomial. Log-prob and entropy come from masked `log_softmax`. Invalid replayed choices give 0 log-prob plus a `valid=False` flag, never NaN.
- **No per-forward device→host sync (L6):** validity flags are returned as a tensor (for tests and telemetry). Action admission is enforced by the native env on `step` (Task 1.4), which fails transactionally. That removes the reference's `_check_flags` sync, where the async fault surfaced, from the hot path.
- **Outputs:**
  - `KaggricultureActions(tokens [E,2,252,12], lengths [E,2])`
  - `ModelActionLogProbs(per_player_entity = per-frame log-probs [E,2,252]; launch / event = zeros)`
  - `ModelActionEntropies` with `components` per slot
  - `ModelOutput` including critic values (2.2)
  - `evaluate_actions` replays `tokens` teacher-forced through the same code path

## Tests (tiny CPU; synthetic tables until 1.2)

1. **Replay invariant (the L6 lesson):** for random observations and sampled actions, `evaluate_actions(obs, sampled).log_probs == forward(obs).log_probs` exactly (eager, FP32), including dense cases (241 actors, 10 orders) and HIRE-capacity cases.
2. **Gumbel exactness:** empirical frequencies of 20k draws match the masked softmax for one slot (chi-square bound).
3. **HIRE coupling:** on a small enumerable case (3 positions, 8 kinds, budgets 0/1/2/3/10), the enumerated joint probability of every final program equals the replayed probability, summing to 1 within 1e-9 (a port of the reference enumeration).
4. **STOP and marginalization:** post-STOP positions contribute 0 log-prob and entropy; `lengths` counts every frame including STOP.
5. **Reserved `unit_target`:** always 0; masked indices never sampled.
6. **Gradient:** a finite-difference check of `evaluate_actions` log-prob with respect to one head weight.
7. **Seat isolation and statelessness** hold through the heads.
8. **Topology:** `actor_input_proj` is `Linear(3D, D)` and Muon-eligible; heads are `OutputProjectionMLP`; embeddings live only under `actor.`; the output-layer gain is 0.01.
9. **Budget:** the full preset has between 6,000,000 and 10,000,000 parameters (owner decision).
10. **Integration (after 1.2):** masks from the native grammar tables decode recorded reference programs identically (oracle), and the replay invariant holds with real tables.

## v2 revisions (Codex review: REVISE; `codex/brief-2.3-review.md` and transcript)

1. **[Blocker, fixed] Unit quantity-mask indexing:** the low-quantity mask is `unit_quantity_low[present, int(high == 0)]` with shape `[2, 2, 32]`, as in the reference. The v1 text `[high, zero]` was wrong. The complete per-stage mask indexing is:

   | Slot | Mask |
   |---|---|
   | `unit_kind` | `unit_kind[live]` |
   | `unit_item` | `unit_item[kind]` |
   | `unit_quantity_present` | `unit_quantity_present[kind]` |
   | `unit_quantity_high` | `unit_quantity_high[present]` |
   | `unit_quantity` | `unit_quantity_low[present, high == 0]` |
   | `market_kind` | `market_kind[available]`, then capacity-corrected |
   | `market_item` | `market_item[kind]` |
   | `market_quantity_high` | `market_quantity[kind]` |
   | `market_quantity` | `market_quantity[kind]` |

   The implementer re-derives every row from `myolie_sampler.rs` and `gpu_sampling_grammar.py`, and a test compares each table against the native plans (Task 1.2).
2. **[Blocker, fixed] Replay validation stays in the model:** PPO and BC replay call `evaluate_actions` without calling native `step`, so the model must validate replayed programs itself, as the reference `_decode` did:
   - every chosen value is inside the prefix-dependent support
   - `lengths` equals the STOP position + 1
   - canonical tokens: unit frame `f` has actor ordinal `f`; reserved `unit_target` = 0; the STOP bit is set exactly on the STOP frame; slots unused by a frame's kind are 0; frames after `lengths` are zero padding

   Violations produce a device-side `valid` tensor. `evaluate_actions` raises in **one** batched check per call, not per field or frame: a single `.all()` synchronization per minibatch, placed after all kernels are enqueued (cheap compared with the update). This check does **not** replace native admission on `step`; both exist. Invalid programs are never silently given 0 log-prob in training.
3. **[Should-fix] Runtime grammar context:** the actor API receives `actor_counts [B]` (own actors per row), `order_limits [B]`, `hire_limit` (config) and `live [B]` (`still_playing`). Unit frames exist for `f < actor_counts`; market positions are available for `p < order_limits`; HIRE capacity uses `actor_counts + exclusive_prefix_hires < hire_limit`.
4. **[Should-fix] L6 replay invariant, strengthened:** the test samples once, saves `tokens`, `lengths` and the per-frame log-probs, then re-evaluates the saved actions (a) with the same batch, (b) split into minibatches of different sizes, and (c) permuted and re-batched. Log-probs must match the **saved** sampling result to ≤ 1e-5 (FP32 eager), including dense cases (241 actors, 10 orders), HIRE-capacity cases and STOP at every position. A second `forward` is never the reference.
5. **[Should-fix] Muon and initialization parity:** follow Isaiah's actor. Declared actor input tables (`market_position`, slot embeddings) are listed explicitly in `get_input_layers` (not excluded by a blanket type rule) and initialized as token parameters (normal, std `D^-0.5`). Head `.out` layers use `_ACTOR_HEAD_INIT_GAIN = 0.01` and are listed in `get_output_layers`. `actor_input_proj` and head `.up` layers are Muon-eligible.
6. **[Should-fix] Per-slot log-probs preserved:** `ModelActionLogProbs.event` carries per-frame, per-slot log-probs `[E,2,252,12]` (zeros for implicit and reserved slots). `per_player_entity = event.sum(-1)`, and a test asserts it. `launch` is zeros. The entropy `components` are per slot.
7. **[Note, pinned] Coupled-Gumbel sequence:**
   - Sampling draws one Gumbel perturbation per market position and computes `raw_kind` by argmax over the initial mask.
   - The **exclusive** raw-HIRE prefix count gives `can_hire`; HIRE is masked where capacity is exhausted, and the argmax is re-taken over the **same perturbed scores** (no redraw).
   - Density evaluation uses the **exclusive final-HIRE prefix count** of the final kinds.
   - The enumeration test (3 positions, 8 kinds, budgets 0/1/2/3/10) checks that the enumerated joint equals the replayed density.
